# AI Credo

How I think about using AI in my learning and my engineering work.

AI is an incredibly powerful tool, and as Uncle Ben said, with great power comes great responsibility. I'm no exception to being a user of AI — believe me, I use it. And yes, it's true AI will do a lot of the coding for us going forward, but being able to prompt it well comes with being able to fully understand what you're doing. That's the responsibility I take seriously in a computer science workspace: still thinking critically, keeping my technical abilities sharp, and never turning in code I couldn't explain on my own. I also keep the economic and environmental costs of AI in mind instead of reaching for it by reflex. I'll take the efficiency, but not when the price is failing to learn something I'll need later.
